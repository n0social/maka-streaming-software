                Virtual camera

                Feed your program layout to Zoom, Discord, or OBS as a camera device.

                This build (preview mode)

                • Start / Stop runs the same program compositor as local recording (gameplay or webcam + mixer audio).
                • PiP, browser overlay, and scene PNG masks are not composited yet.
                • Other apps cannot select “Maka” as a camera until a driver is bundled.

                Windows fallback

                1. Install OBS Studio or the standalone OBS Virtual Camera package.
                2. Start OBS Virtual Camera from OBS, or register the OBS virtual cam DLL.
                3. In your meeting app, choose OBS Virtual Camera as the video source.
                4. Point OBS at Maka with a Window Capture or Display Capture of the program monitor.
